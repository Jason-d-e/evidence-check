# DemoClub Learning Centre: venue and access guide

Version 2, effective 1 December 2026. DemoClub is fictional.

## Getting there

The DemoClub Learning Centre is at 12 Jalan Contoh, Kuala Lumpur. The nearest station is Contoh LRT station, about 400 m from the main entrance. Buses 23 and 41 stop outside the building.

## Parking

The centre has no visitor parking. The nearest public car park is at Plaza Contoh, about 300 m away. DemoClub does not pay for parking.

## Accessibility

The north entrance is step-free, and a lift serves every floor. There are accessible toilets on each floor. A quiet room, Room 2.04, is open during all event hours. If you need any other support, tell us in the registration form or write to access@democlub.invalid at least 7 days before the event.

## Check-in

Check-in is at the main desk on the ground floor. Staff scan the QR code from your confirmation email and check your student ID card. Every time you leave and re-enter the building during the event, scan your QR code at the desk.

## Lockers and lost property

There are 40 lockers on the ground floor, available on a first-come, first-served basis; bring your own padlock. DemoClub is not responsible for items left in the hall. Lost property is kept at the main desk for 30 days.

## Wi-Fi

The guest network is DemoClub-Guest. The password is given at check-in and is not published.
