# DemoClub Build Weekend 2027: participant handbook

Version 3, effective 1 December 2026. DemoClub and everything in this handbook are fictional.

## 1. Dates and venue

Build Weekend runs from Saturday 16 January 2027 at 09:00 to Sunday 17 January 2027 at 17:00 MYT. It takes place in Hall B of the DemoClub Learning Centre, 12 Jalan Contoh, Kuala Lumpur. Doors open at 08:15 on Saturday and at 08:00 on Sunday. The building closes at 23:00 on Saturday night, and staying overnight is not allowed. Build Weekend is an in-person event only; there is no online track.

Each team gets one 15-minute slot with a mentor in each mentor round. Demos are 4 minutes per team, followed by 2 minutes of questions from the judges.

## 3. Who can take part

Build Weekend is open to currently enrolled university and college students aged 18 or older. People who graduated in the last 12 months may join as mentors, but not as participants. There are 120 participant places. Teams have 2 to 4 members; solo entries are not accepted. Participants without a team are matched into teams at team formation on Saturday morning. Each team must include at least one member who can write code.

## 4. Registration and deposit

Registration opens on 1 December 2026 and closes on 3 January 2027 at 23:59 MYT, or earlier if all 120 places are taken. Each participant registers individually through the DemoClub event form, even when they already have a team. A place is confirmed only after a refundable deposit of RM 50 is paid. Confirmation emails are sent within 3 working days of payment.

The deposit is refunded within 14 days after the event to every participant who checks in on both days. A participant who misses either day loses the deposit. A participant who cancels by 9 January 2027 at 23:59 MYT gets the deposit back in full. After that, the deposit is refunded only with a medical certificate.

## 5. Travel grants

Up to 20 travel grants are available for participants who live more than 100 km from the venue. A grant covers bus or train fares up to RM 300 per participant; flights, taxis and fuel are not covered. Apply through the travel grant form by 20 December 2026; decisions are emailed by 28 December 2026. Grants are paid by bank transfer after the event, against original receipts, within 30 days.

## 6. What to submit

Each team submits one project through the submission page before submission closes. A submission must include a link to a public code repository, a demo video of no more than 3 minutes, and a README that explains how to run the project. All work must be started at the event. Teams may use open-source libraries and publicly available datasets, and must list them in the README. Teams may use AI tools, and must state in the README which tools they used and for what. Late submissions are not judged.

## 7. Judging and prizes

Five judges score every project on four criteria: usefulness (30%), technical execution (30%), design (20%) and presentation (20%). A tie is broken by the usefulness score.

| Prize | Amount |
|---|---|
| First place | RM 3,000 |
| Second place | RM 1,500 |
| Best Accessibility Award | RM 500 |

There is no third-place prize. Prize money is shared equally among team members and paid by bank transfer within 30 days after the event. A team can win at most one prize.

## 8. Food

Lunch and dinner are provided on Saturday, and breakfast and lunch on Sunday. All meals include vegetarian and halal options. Tell us about food allergies in the registration form by 3 January 2027.

## 9. What to bring

Bring your own laptop and charger; DemoClub cannot lend laptops. Every table has power sockets, and Wi-Fi is provided. Bring your student ID card and the QR code from your confirmation email; both are checked at check-in.

## 10. Conduct, safety and photos

All participants follow the DemoClub code of conduct. To report a problem, speak to any staff member in a green T-shirt or write to conduct@democlub.invalid; the conduct team replies within 24 hours. The event is photographed. Participants who do not want to appear in photos can ask for a red lanyard at check-in.

## 11. Ownership

Teams keep full ownership of their projects. DemoClub may show project names and screenshots in its event recap, unless a team opts out in the submission form.
