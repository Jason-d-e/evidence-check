# DemoClub season report 2025

DemoClub is fictional. This report was published in December 2025.

## Members

Membership reached 43,000 people by the end of the season. About one-third of members joined during 2025.

## Checking results

Every result was checked twice. Agreement between the two passes was 0.996. None of the 48 scoring runs failed.

## Tools

Answers were scored with ranker-v3.2. Speech was transcribed with echo-asr-2025-08-28, at US$0.10 per million tokens. The p90 latency was 1.2 seconds.

## Calendar

The spring meetup ran on Saturday 12 and Sunday 13 April 2025. The autumn meetup was held in October 2025.

## Spending

Venue hire cost RM 4,350 and catering cost RM 2,180.
