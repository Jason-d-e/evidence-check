# DemoClub workshop information

Version 2, effective 2026-11-03. DemoClub is fictional.

## event

The Intro to AI Tools workshop takes place on 12 November 2026 from 19:00 to 20:30 MYT. It is an online workshop. The capacity is 24 participants. Participation is free.

## registration

Registration closes on 11 November 2026 at 18:00 MYT. A person registers through the DemoClub event form. A confirmation email is sent by 11 November at 20:00 MYT. Submitting the form does not guarantee a place. If all 24 places are filled, later applicants join a waiting list.

## eligibility

The workshop is open to currently enrolled university students aged 18 or older. No prior programming experience is required. Participants need a laptop with an up-to-date web browser and an internet connection. There is no certificate for attending this workshop.
